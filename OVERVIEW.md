# AshTec Crew Hub

The Ashford School Tech Crew platform for optional events such as The Addams Family (TAF) and High School Musical (HSM).

- **Crew members** sign in with their school email and get a one-time code. They can also edit their own first and last name, year, school email and top two preferred roles from **My Profile**; everything else is admin-managed, and editing is locked while previewing somebody else. Year is limited to the non-Staff years: picking Staff is the admin-only staff access level, so it cannot be self-assigned, and the server rejects it independently of the UI. Changing your school email keeps the old address working as a backup so a typo can't lock you out. Each show asks them Yes/No/Maybe. If they say yes, they then confirm which rehearsals and performances they'll attend. Saying "can't go" needs a reason of at least 8 words. Anyone who says No to a show is automatically marked "Not Attending Event" for everything in it. Anyone attending no performances is automatically dropped from rehearsals. Crew members also pick their top two preferred roles.
- **Admins** manage shows and their rehearsals and performances (type, kind, date or TBC, meet time, timings, things to bring, importance, and response deadlines or "unknown"). They can see expected attendance and manage the crew list: roles, heads of area, member type (Teacher/Expert/Normal Member), admin access, and notes. They can also record responses given in person.
- Roles: Stage Left, Stage Right, Microphone Management, Lighting, Sound.
- **Actors** don't sign up for events. They only see the stage layout, where they can find and email the head of each area.
- Admins can send reminder emails and messages; group messages show as one collapsible entry in the email log. Every automated email carries the same footer explaining it was sent automatically.
- **Calendar** (`/calendar`) is open to every member, not just admins: a shared month grid of every rehearsal and performance across all shows, so people can spot clashes. Filters for show and type, plus a hidden-events toggle that only admins get because hidden events are never sent to anyone else. Events without a confirmed date are listed underneath the grid rather than dropped, since a month view cannot place them.
- **Hidden shows and events** stay fully in the admin area but are removed from what members see, including their forms, reminders and stage layout. Hidden items are marked with a crossed-out eye wherever an admin sees them.
- **Crew filters**: the Crew page filters by search, year, member type, role (assigned or head of), and flags for admins, maintainers, preview accounts, staff and outstanding forms. The result count updates live and one button clears everything.
- **Help & feedback** (bottom-left button): bug reports and feature requests go to chosen **maintainers**; general support goes to all admins who aren't staff/teachers. Admins track everything in the Support tab.
- **Refer to opencode**: a flag on bug reports and feature requests that marks a ticket as coding work. It is deliberately inert: setting it sends no email, schedules no job and notifies nobody. It only makes the ticket easy to find later. It shows as a badge on the Support list and the ticket page, and the list can filter to just the flagged ones. General support tickets cannot carry it, since they are questions rather than pieces of work.
- **Support tickets** get a full thread at `/admin/support/:ticketId`: public replies to the member, private notes the member never sees, status, assignee, a shared scratchpad and deletion. Maintainers are alerted again after 12 hours of silence and every 12 hours after that until somebody replies. Replies land in the replying maintainer's inbox with a warning that direct email replies are not monitored, so the ticket is the record. There is no inbound email ingestion: transfer anything useful from the inbox into a note.
- **Preview accounts**: pretend people admins can create and "preview as" to see the app from a member's view. They can't sign in or get emails.

- **Navigation**: member pages sit in the top bar, and every admin page is grouped behind a single **Admin** dropdown (Manage Events, Crew, Emails, Support) so the bar stays short. The trigger shows how many support tickets are waiting on you.
- **Guided tour**: a first-run spotlight tour greets each member once. It dims the page and punches a hole around one real control at a time, and navigation steps wait for an actual click. It only ever clicks read-only navigation, never anything that writes, so it cannot save a profile or submit a response on someone's behalf. Content is filtered by role, so an Actor is never shown show-response buttons they do not have. Progress is stored per member in the browser, so the tour runs once per device rather than once per account, and the `?` button in the header reopens it at any time.
